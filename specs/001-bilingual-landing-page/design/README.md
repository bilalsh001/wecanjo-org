"We Can Organization Website" (https://stitch.google.com/projects/8579039485054551110).

depends on the Tailwind CDN, so the site re-implements them in semantic HTML and CSS (see

## Differences the build corrects

These come from the spec clarifications and the constitution:

- The Arabic organization name is "جمعية نحن نستطيع"; the designs show the earlier draft
  "منظمة نحن نستطيع".
- The header uses the real "WC" mark (56–64 px tall) beside the typeset name, and the footer shows
  the complete logo.
- Arabic founder badges use Arabic initials.
- Helper sentences Stitch invented in the Contact section (for example "Direct lines for
  administrative support and regional community desks" and "We welcome collaborations, inquiries,
  and dialogue…") are not used: they are not approved copy. Placeholders say "Coming soon" only.
