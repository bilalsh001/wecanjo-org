About us

Who we are

We are an organization dedicated to serving both women and youth through a variety of programs. We are registered as a local Non-Governmental Organization (NGO) or Community-Based Organization (CBO) in Jordan and are working to expand our impact and create meaningful change on multiple levels.

Our mission

How we create change

To build a dynamic platform that supports, encourages and empowers youth and women to thrive socially and economically at all levels.

By leveraging the collective strength of our ecosystem, we believe that individual success drives community prosperity.
